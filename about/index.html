About Us

Home Comfort for Dunnville and the Lake Erie Shore

From the bridges over the Grand down to the Lake Erie shore, we keep households across Dunnville and Haldimand County warm through lake-effect cold snaps and cool when July turns sticky off the lake.

Our Story

A Dunnville Crew, Built One Call at a Time

Dunnville Heating & Cooling is run by neighbours who actually live in town, folks who know what a lake-effect snow squall does to an aging furnace, and how badly a humid August stretch can swamp an air conditioner that was never sized for this climate.

Dunnville sits where the Grand River finally empties into Lake Erie, a river town stitched together by its bridges and ringed by Haldimand farmland, famous locally as the Mudcat Capital of the World. The seasons here hit hard: lake-effect snow rolls in off Erie and settles into the older homes near the river flats, the humidity climbs the moment the lake warms up, and the shoulder weeks can swing from heating to cooling inside a few days. That is the climate our furnaces, air conditioners, and heat pumps are actually built to handle.

The housing around here throws curveballs a generic out-of-town contractor will miss. Century homes near downtown Dunnville, post-war bungalows set back from the river, lake places out toward Port Maitland and Rock Point, and farmhouses across the open Haldimand flats all heat and cool differently. We read the house in front of us, not just the model number on the unit, so the system we install or repair genuinely fits how the building breathes.

That covers the full spread of residential comfort work: furnace installation and repair for the deep winter, central and ductless air conditioning for humid summers, heat pumps for households chasing lower year-round energy bills, fireplace installation, thermostat upgrades, and duct cleaning. One local crew, one number to call, from the first visit right through the follow-up.

Our Commitment

Our Standard on Every Service Call

Every visit starts with a real diagnosis, not a sales pitch. We walk you through what we found, lay out the honest options in plain language, and quote the job before any tool comes out of the van. If a repair holds up today, that is what we recommend; if a worn part is going to put you back in the cold come February, we will say so straight. No mystery line items appear on the final invoice.

We are licensed, insured, and trained on the equipment actually sitting in Haldimand County basements, from older mid-efficiency furnaces still doing their best, to the high-efficiency and heat-pump systems we install ourselves, and every brand of air conditioner the lake humidity gets to test. Same-day and emergency service is available, because furnaces and air conditioners do not fail on a schedule, and a freezing night or a sweltering, sticky one is no time to be left waiting.
